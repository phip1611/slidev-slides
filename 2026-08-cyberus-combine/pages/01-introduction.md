# Familiar Situations?

<v-clicks depth="2">

- A conference talk begins. The topic sounds great, and the speaker starts
  talking... but it quickly becomes harder to pay attention.
- You wanted to shine, but someone stole the spotlight:
  - For example, at work, conferences, meetups, bars, or student parties
  - Often, the other person is not necessarily more competent but **confidently
    comes across as if they are**.
- Delivery is important!

</v-clicks>

# Room for Improvements All of us Have

<v-clicks depth="2">

- Monotone voice
- "Umm" / "Ähhm"
- Being afraid to pause!
- We don't vary enough with: volume, tonality, speed, pitch
- "It feels unnatural / this is not my voice"
  - Technically our voices can do much more than we think, we just never learned
    to ...
  - Use certain unfamiliar dimensions of it,
  - Be comfortable with it

</v-clicks>

---
---

# Specific Steps

<v-clicks depth="2">

- No more "Umm" / "Ähhm"
- Don't be afraid to pause
- Vary: volume, tonality, speed, pitch
- Give a talk (5 min) and record yourself:
  - Rewatch it without sound and analyze
  - Just listen to it and analyze
  - Feel the cringe! But it's getting better!

</v-clicks>
