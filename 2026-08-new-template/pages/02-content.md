---

# 2.1 Reveal the shape of an idea

<v-clicks depth="2">

- Start with the conclusion
  - Give the audience a place to stand
- Reveal only the supporting detail you need
  - Keep the conversation moving
- End with the consequence

</v-clicks>

---
layout: default
---

# 2.2 Footnotes for the fine print

<v-clicks>

- Keep the claim on the slide short<sup>1</sup>
- Move sources and definitions to the bottom<sup>2</sup>

</v-clicks>
